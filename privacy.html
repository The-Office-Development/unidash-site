Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

UniDash ("the App", "we", "us") is a university cafeteria pre-order service consisting of a student app and a staff/admin app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it is protected, and the rights you have under the Jordanian Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 (PDPL).

Data controller: The Office Development, Amman, Jordan
Contact for privacy requests: privacy@unidash.food

Card payments (including Apple Pay and Google Pay) are processed by Mastercard Payment Gateway Services (MPGS) via its secure hosted payment page. Your full card number is entered directly with the gateway and is not stored by the App; if you choose to save a card for reuse, we retain only a non-sensitive gateway token and the card's brand, expiry, and last four digits to display it. Wallet top-ups are performed in person by cafeteria administration.

We do not collect your device's location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising or profiling of any kind.

2. Legal basis for processing

Under the PDPL we process your data:

  • To perform the service you request: placing, preparing, and handing over your orders, and operating your wallet (PDPL Art. 6(a)).
  • With your consent: push notifications (you can revoke this at any time in your device settings without affecting your ability to order).
  • For our legitimate operational needs: crash diagnostics and fraud prevention, limited to the minimum data necessary.

3. Who can see your data

  • Cafeteria staff see the name, order contents, pickup time, and payment method of orders placed at their cafeteria, only what is needed to prepare and hand over your order.
  • Cafeteria administrators can additionally view order history and manage wallet balances (credits are logged with the administrator's identity).
  • Students can only ever see their own profile, orders, wallet, and notifications. Access is enforced server-side with row-level security, not just in the app.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where a competent Jordanian authority requires disclosure by law.

5. How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS); the App refuses cleartext connections and additionally pins the backend's certificate chain.
  • Database access is gated by row-level security policies tied to your authenticated identity.
  • Your login session is stored in the device's encrypted keystore (Keychain / EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Wallet balance changes are executed by atomic server-side procedures and recorded in an immutable transaction log.
  • Release builds are code-obfuscated.
  • Staff and admin accounts are created manually by administrators only. There is no self-signup for privileged roles.

6. Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Order and wallet transaction records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention, then deleted or irreversibly anonymised.
  • Push tokens are deleted when you log out or uninstall the App.
  • Crash data is retained per Firebase Crashlytics' standard 90-day rolling window.

7. Your rights under the PDPL

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (name and profile details can be edited in the App);
  • Erase your data by requesting account deletion (see below);
  • Withdraw consent to optional processing (e.g. disable push notifications) at any time;
  • Object to processing that is not necessary for providing the service;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Council at the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship (MoDEE).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@unidash.food. We will respond within the timeframes required by the PDPL and will verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account and personal data at any time: in the app via Profile → Delete account, or by request (see the account deletion page). Deletion is irreversible; any remaining wallet balance is forfeited. Order and transaction records we must keep for accounting are retained in anonymised form.

9. Children

The App is intended for university students and staff. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the "Last updated" date. For material changes we will notify you in the App before they take effect.
